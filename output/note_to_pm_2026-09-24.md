**Three honest notes on the screen itself.** It fires about twice a month, not weekly the
way you described it, so it's tighter than your eye. Over 76 past candidates the lean did
separate, with the ones it liked clearing their range 14% of the time against 4% for the
rest, though on this sample size that's suggestive rather than settled. And the whole pool
clears its range less often than an average name does on an average Thursday (11% against
17%), because after a big move the range is wide and the bar is further away. On next-day
returns these names do better than average, not worse.

**What I'd change first:** only run two-day consolidations. They go again 20% of the time
against a 20% base rate, while three and four-day setups run at 4% against 15 to 17%.

**What I'd test next:** whether the event day was accumulation or short covering. I think
that split matters more than anything price-based in here.
